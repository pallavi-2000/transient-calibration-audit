ALeRCE (Balanced Random Forest, lc_classifier v1.1.13)
  Architecture: Balanced Random Forest
  Classes: 4 (SNIa, SNIbc, SNII, SLSN)
  Sample: 1114 objects
  Accuracy: 0.742
  Mean confidence: 0.471
  Direction: underconfident
  ECE: 0.271 [0.249, 0.296]
  Temperature: T=0.357
  Post-calibration ECE: 0.097 (65% improvement)
  Finding: Systematic underconfidence inverts Guo et al. (2017) norm

Fink Random Forest (rf_snia_vs_nonia)
  Type: Binary (SN Ia vs not)
  Sample: 1237 objects
  Zero scores (abstentions): 93.9%
  Finding: Operational regime mismatch, not a calibration failure -- RF is an early-time classifier (Leoni et al. 2022) evaluated on well-evolved BTS objects outside its design regime; ECE excluded from calibration analysis (see figures/main_updated.tex sec:fink_rf)

Fink SuperNNova (snn_snia_vs_nonia)
  Type: Binary (SN Ia vs not)
  Sample: 1237 objects
  Zero scores (abstentions): 35.9%
  Finding: Implicit selective classification, class-dependent abstention. Conditional ECE (accepted set only) = 0.183, reduced to 0.051 by T=3.65 -- but T>1 eliminates all p>=0.8 predictions (operationally null at high thresholds; see results/fink_snn_conditional_analysis.json)

NEEDLE (Sheng et al. 2024)
  Architecture: Hybrid CNN + DNN
  Classes: SN, SLSN-I, TDE
  Sample: 429 predictions, 278 objects
  NOTE: object-level dedup (see results/needle_dedup_results.json) is PRIMARY in the paper (ECE=0.048, N=278 unique objects). The model-instance numbers below (N=429 predictions) are the paper's sensitivity analysis.
  Accuracy: 0.841
  Mean confidence: 0.851
  Aggregate ECE (model-instance): 0.073
    SN: ECE=0.171, acc=0.929, conf=0.870
    SLSN-I: ECE=0.118, acc=0.690, conf=0.846
    TDE: ECE=0.070, acc=0.801, conf=0.823
  Global T=1.552: ECE 0.126 -> 0.169 (WORSENS)
  Per-class T: {'SN': 1.852, 'SLSN-I': 2.034, 'TDE': 0.539}
  Per-class ECE: 0.167
  Finding: Aggregate ECE (0.050) masks class-asymmetric miscalibration. SLSN-I is overconfident (driven by inverse-frequency class weighting; Sheng et al. 2024 Table 1 gives SN=5237, TDE=64 training examples, ~80:1), TDE is underconfident. Global temperature scaling worsens ECE because it cannot simultaneously soften and sharpen different classes.

======================================================================
KEY TAKEAWAYS
======================================================================
1. No production classifier publishes calibration metrics
2. ALeRCE: fixable underconfidence (T=0.36 reduces ECE by ~65%), 21x operational gain at p>0.8 under held-out CV
3. Fink RF: operational regime mismatch (93.9% abstain outside its early-time design regime) -- not structurally broken
4. Fink SNN: implicit selective classification; T-scaling improves conditional ECE but is operationally null at high thresholds
5. NEEDLE: aggregate ECE (object-level, primary) masks class-asymmetric miscalibration
6. Inverse-frequency class weighting (~80:1 SN:TDE, Sheng et al. 2024 Table 1) distorts probabilities
7. The failure of temperature scaling for NEEDLE is itself publishable
